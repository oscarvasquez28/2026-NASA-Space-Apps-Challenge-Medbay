# Countermeasure & Accelerated Aging Prescriber

## Pathophysiology

Skeletal unloading and absence of hydrostatic gradients accelerate phenotypes of biological aging:
- **Bone Demineralization:** Trabecular bone resorption at 1.0-1.5% per month, risking osteopenia (T < -1.0) and severe osteoporosis (T $\le$ -2.5).
- **Postural Muscle Atrophy:** Rapid loss of slow-twitch postural fiber volume, particularly soleus and gastrocnemius.
- **Metabolic Shift:** Impaired skeletal muscle glucose disposal leading to spaceflight insulin resistance and dysglycemia (blood sugar > 110 mg/dL).
- **Spinal Elongation:** Paraspinal disc expansion (5-7 cm height increase) causing lumbar radiculopathy.

## Thresholds & Actions

- **Critical:** BMD T-score $\le -2.5$, blood glucose $\ge 180.0$ mg/dL, or radicular neurological deficit.
  - *Severity:* `critical` (`TIER_3_GROUND_SURGEON`)
  - *Protocols:* Halt High-Impact Axial Loading Protocol, Anti-Resorptive / Bisphosphonate Clinical Review, Acute Hyperglycemia Insulin / Glycemic Stabilization Protocol, Urgent Ground Surgeon Spine & Metabolic Telemedicine Consult.
- **Caution:** BMD T-score $< -1.0$, blood glucose $> 110.0$ mg/dL, resting HR $> 85$ bpm, or muscle weakness/cramps.
  - *Severity:* `caution` (`TIER_2_CMO_REVIEW`)
  - *Protocols:*
    1. **Zero T2 Exercise Regimen Protocol:** 45 min ARED heavy resistive loading (squats, deadlifts) + 30 min CEVIS interval training.
    2. **Food Physiology Countermeasure Protocol:** Reduced sodium (<2000 mg/d), high protein (1.2-1.5 g/kg), Vitamin D3 (1000 IU), calcium citrate.
    3. **Space Gardening Daily Cultivation Task:** 20 min harvesting fresh microgreens in Veggie/APH for antioxidant carotenoids and polyphenols.
    4. **Lumbar Fetal Sleep Positioning Protocol:** Spinal tether alignment.
- **Nominal:** BMD T-score $\ge -1.0$, blood glucose 70-110 mg/dL, no muscle atrophy or spinal pain.
  - *Protocols:* Nominal 2.5-Hour Daily Exercise Countermeasure Block (ARED + T2/CEVIS), Standard Artemis Food System Maintenance.
